PuraEvents Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 16, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information PuraEvents (“the app”, “we”) handles and how.

1. Summary

PuraEvents is offline-first: your events, reminders, and appearance settings are stored on this device, and no account is needed. We don't run our own servers or analytics; crash reports go to Sentry (section 4). The free version shows ads through Google AdMob, which collects some device data to do so (see section 4); PuraEvents Pro has no ads. Purchases are verified through RevenueCat.

2. Information Stored On Your Device

Events you create (title, date/time, timezone, category, notes, reminders, appearance, and any optional Share message or sender name), widgets you design (photos and style settings), and app preferences (theme, language, calendar system) are stored locally on your device. It leaves your device only when you export it yourself (see "Your Choices" below), or through your phone's own backup: on Android, the system's Auto Backup saves the app's events and settings (not photos) to your own Google account, and on iPhone they are part of your iCloud device backup. Those backups are run by Google or Apple under your account and your phone's backup settings; we never receive them.

3. Photos You Add

If you pick a photo from your device's photo library for a custom widget, the app copies it into its own local storage so it keeps working after you close the picker. We never upload that photo anywhere.

4. Third-Party Services We Use

Pexels API: the Events tab background photo and the Pro category-photo gallery are fetched from Pexels (pexels.com), using only a city name (derived from your device's timezone, not GPS — we never request location access) or a category keyword as the search term. Notifications: reminders are scheduled locally using your operating system's own notification system — we don't run a push server and never see or store your reminder content remotely. App Store / Google Play: a Pro purchase is handled entirely by Apple or Google; we don't receive or store your payment card details. RevenueCat (revenuecat.com): checks whether you have an active Pro purchase, using your store purchase receipts and a random app user ID it creates — not your name or email. Google AdMob (free version only): to show, measure, and limit ads and to prevent ad fraud, Google's Mobile Ads SDK collects your device's advertising ID, IP address, device and app information, and how you interact with ads. Depending on your region and settings these ads may be personalized. Google handles this data under its own policy: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites. Sentry (sentry.io, servers in the EU): when the app crashes or hits an error, a report with the technical details (which code failed, app version, phone model and OS version) is sent so we can fix it. It doesn't include your events, notes, or account details, and we don't send your IP address with it.

5. Data We Do Not Collect

We don't run analytics, don't ask for your name, email, or location, and don't sell your personal data. The advertising data described in section 4 is collected by Google's SDK in the free version, not by us. If any of this changes, this policy — and your platform's privacy labels — will be updated first.

6. Your Choices

Export: Settings → Data & Privacy → Export shares all of your events as a file at any time. Delete: since your data lives on this device, clearing the app's data or uninstalling it deletes it there; copies in your phone's own Google or iCloud backup are managed (and can be deleted) in that account's backup settings. Access/correction: view and edit every event directly in the app at any time. Ads: you can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalized ads, in your phone's settings (Android: Settings → Google → Ads, or Privacy → Ads; iPhone: Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking). Upgrading to Pro removes ads.

7. Children's Privacy

PuraEvents isn't directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age required in your country), and we don't knowingly collect personal information from children.

8. International Users (GDPR / CCPA)

Your events stay on your device, and we don't process your personal data on servers of our own. The services in section 4 (Google AdMob, RevenueCat, Sentry, Pexels, and Apple/Google for purchases) process the limited data described there. If you're in the EU/UK or California and have questions about your rights under GDPR or the CCPA, contact us using the details below.

9. Changes To This Policy

We may update this policy as the app changes — for example when cloud sync, analytics, or new subscription products are added. We'll update the date above and, for material changes, note it in the app's release notes.
